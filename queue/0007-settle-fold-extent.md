## Context

Infinite creases are the biggest open question in the direction (`AGENTS.md` §"Open
design questions", `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §"What is deliberately still open"). A fold
here guts a structure over there, and that is kept so it can be *felt* before it is
designed away. Barrier-scoped folds are the leading candidate; spans bound a strip's
width, not its length.
